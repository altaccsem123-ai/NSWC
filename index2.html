Command Overview

Naval Special Warfare Group (NSWG) provides a tightly-structured simulation environment modeled after real-world Special Mission Unit tasking. The unit replicates the doctrinal tempo, target development processes, and intelligence integration of NSW operations while maintaining a professional and mature standard.

This command emphasizes intelligence-driven mission sets, precision tasking, and inter-unit integration with elements representing the 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment and Air Force Special Tactics. No filler missions. No sandbox raids. All operations stem from actionable intel, validated through our internal J2 node.

Applicants are reviewed based on operational mindset, discipline, and willingness to uphold NSWG methodology. Skill is trainable. Judgment is not. Assignment is selective and based on billet availability.

All inquiries route directly to SOCM R. Evans via secured comms.

Operational Doctrine & Mission Structure

NSWG missions are not randomized engagements. Each action originates from an intelligence catalyst — SIGINT intercept, ISR anomaly, human source reporting, or DOMEX exploitation.

Target Development Cycle

  • SIGINT: correlates comms patterns, digital emissions, and encrypted channels.
  • ISR: establishes persistent surveillance and pattern-of-life validation.
  • DOMEX: triages captured media, biometric matches, and device forensics.
  • J2 Review: compiles threat matrices and confirms identity logic.

Joint Planning Cell Output

  • Ingress/egress route modeling
  • Alternate COA validation
  • Environmental risk review
  • Asset synchronization (STS, 160th, ISR nodes)
  • OPORD drafting with precise tasking

Lifecycle of NSWG Operations

  • 1. Trigger Event
  • 2. Validation
  • 3. WARNO
  • 4. OPORD & Rehearsal
  • 5. Execution
  • 6. SSE / DOMEX
  • 7. AAR
